# LifeGame

## Concept
A mix of BitLife and Skyrim. You start as a fetus and live an entire life — birth, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, old age, and death. Combines BitLife's life-simulation choices with Skyrim's open-world RPG mechanics (exploration, combat, skills, quests).


- When police is called, it must be a specific NPC who calls the police then they raise their arm to call
- The game needs to feel like a real life simulation where you live a life, either a very good righteous life, or a fun popular life, or a boring life, or a very evil life.
- Include the things from the very worst of humanity (murder, torture, stealing, arson, rape, betrayal, abuse, addiction, suicide, genocide, racism) to the very best (helping, achievement, gaining money, success, goals, expansion, etc)
- all choices must have consequences


- Basically the game is like a mix of GTA, Skyrim, and Bitlife. It's like GTA/Skyrim but you start from birth and go until death, and you can't respawn you just die and then have to start a new life.
